# Musk Insights Tracker

Track and analyze Elon Musk's social, financial, and political influence.

## Core Capabilities
- **Social Media Monitoring**: X/Twitter activity analysis, engagement patterns, narrative tracking
- **Financial Impact**: Tesla, SpaceX, xAI, Neuralink, X Corp performance and market influence
- **Political Influence**: Policy positions, regulatory interactions, government relationships
- **Trend Analysis**: Identify patterns in Musk's public statements and their market/social effects
- **Sentiment Tracking**: Public and media sentiment shifts correlated with Musk's activities

## Tracking Dimensions
- **X/Twitter Activity**: Post frequency, topics, engagement metrics, viral moments
- **Company News**: Tesla deliveries, SpaceX launches, xAI releases, Neuralink milestones
- **Policy Impact**: Regulatory developments, government contracts, political statements
- **Market Reaction**: Stock price movements, crypto mentions, market sentiment shifts
- **Media Coverage**: Mainstream and alternative media narrative analysis

## Workflow
1. Monitor recent activity across tracked dimensions
2. Identify significant events, statements, or pattern shifts
3. Analyze impact on relevant markets, policies, or public discourse
4. Provide contextual analysis with historical comparison
5. Flag emerging trends and potential implications

## Pitfalls
- Separate verified facts from speculation and rumors
- Acknowledge the rapid pace of Musk-related news cycles
- Consider multiple perspectives, not just bullish or bearish
- Trackers can become outdated quickly — note data freshness

## Inputs

- `dimensions` (select) — X/Twitter activity (frequency, topics, engagement, viral moments) · company news (Tesla deliveries, SpaceX launches, xAI releases, Neuralink milestones) · policy impact (positions, regulatory interactions, government relationships) · market reaction (stocks, crypto mentions, sentiment) · media coverage (mainstream + alternative narratives)
- `timeframe` — recent activity window; note data freshness (trackers go stale fast in Musk news cycles)

## Outputs

- Impact analysis per dimension with historical comparison and contextual framing
- Trend analysis: patterns in public statements and their market/social effects; sentiment tracking correlated with activities
- Emerging trends flagged with potential implications — multiple perspectives (never just bullish or bearish), verified facts separated from speculation and rumors
- Format: insights report with freshness timestamps

## Example

Musk posts about X payments expansion → monitor: post frequency, topics, engagement metrics, viral spread → company news: X Corp statements → policy: regulatory reactions → market: crypto/stock price movements → media: narrative framing → analysis: historical comparison to prior payments remarks, sentiment shift, implications — verified facts labeled separately from speculation, data freshness timestamped (Musk news cycles move fast).
