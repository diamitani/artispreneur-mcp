# Unleash Sales Greatness

## Overview
Provides practical, empathetic sales training and growth strategies rooted in the unleashyoursalesgreatness.com methodology. Emphasizes mindset shifts, authentic communication, and sustainable performance over quick-fix tactics.

## When to Use
- Coaching a rep through a specific deal challenge or skill gap
- Designing a sales training session or workshop
- Building a personal growth plan for a sales professional
- Shifting team culture from transactional to relationship-driven selling
- Overcoming mindset blocks: fear of rejection, imposter syndrome, call reluctance
- Developing authentic talk tracks that don't sound scripted

## How It Works
1. Start with the person, not the problem — understand their context, motivation, and blockers.
2. Apply the Unleash framework: awareness → belief → action → results.
3. Provide specific, actionable strategies — not generic advice.
4. Balance tactical skill-building with mindset coaching.
5. End with a concrete commitment: one thing they'll do differently next call.

## Steps
1. **Discovery** — Ask: "What's the specific situation you're facing? What have you tried? What's holding you back?"
2. **Mindset check** — Identify limiting beliefs (e.g., "I don't want to be pushy," "They're too senior for me"). Challenge them with reframes.
3. **Skill diagnosis** — Pinpoint the specific skill gap: discovery questions, objection handling, closing, prospecting, etc.
4. **Strategy design** — Build an actionable approach with scripts, frameworks, or conversation maps tailored to their scenario.
5. **Practice** — Role-play the approach. Give direct, kind feedback. Iterate.
6. **Commitment** — Have them articulate one specific action they'll take before the next session.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Skipping mindset**: Giving tactical advice without addressing the underlying mindset leads to surface-level change at best.
- **Over-scripting**: Authenticity wins deals. Scripts are scaffolds, not straitjackets.
- **One-and-done coaching**: Real behavior change requires follow-up, practice, and accountability.
- **Ignoring the human**: Sales is about helping people solve problems. Frameworks that forget this feel manipulative.
- **Comparing to top performers**: Growth is personal. Measure progress against their own baseline, not the team MVP.

## Inputs

- `situation` -- the rep's specific situation: a deal challenge, a skill gap, a mindset block (fear of rejection, imposter syndrome, call reluctance)
- `history` -- what they've tried, what's holding them back

## Outputs

- A coaching arc: discovery -> mindset check (limiting beliefs identified and reframed) -> skill diagnosis (discovery questions, objection handling, closing, prospecting) -> tailored strategy (scripts, frameworks, conversation maps) -> role-play with direct, kind feedback -> one concrete commitment for the next call

## Example

Call reluctance: "I don't want to be pushy" surfaced and reframed -> discovery-question skill gap diagnosed -> authentic talk track built (scaffold, not script) -> role-played twice with feedback -> commitment: "open the next 3 calls with the new discovery question".
